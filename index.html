A Message from the Secretariat

Esteemed Delegates, Faculty Advisors, and Fellow Brathwaite Students,

It is our distinct privilege to welcome you to the Harold M. Brathwaite Secondary School Model United Nations program. Model UN at HMBSS is built on the belief that students should not merely observe international affairs, but actively engage with the complexities of global diplomacy, international law, and multilateral negotiation.

Every Monday during lunch in Rooms 301 and 302, our members gather to refine their public speaking, master parliamentary procedure, and debate pressing global issues. Alongside preparing our school delegation for external conferences, our 15-member Secretariat is hard at work organizing our own conference, Brathwaite Model United Nations (BMUN).

Whether you are stepping up to the podium for your very first speech or drafting complex crisis directives, you have a place in our delegation.

Dual Program Structure

The School Delegation & BMUN

Our organization operates both as Harold M. Brathwaite’s competitive Model UN team and as the host Secretariat for Brathwaite Model United Nations.

Pillar I • School Delegation Mondays at Lunch • Rms 301 & 302

HMBSS Model UN Team

Every Monday during lunch in Rooms 301 and 302, delegates come together for structured training sessions, practice simulations, and foreign policy workshops led by our USG of Training team. We prepare Brathwaite students of all experience levels to excel at regional and high school conferences.

  • 01 Weekly Lunchtime Sessions: Held every Monday during lunch in Rooms 301 & 302.
  • 02 Comprehensive Curriculum: 8 official HMBSS training guides covering RoP, Speeches, Double Del, Foreign Policy, Crisis, Position Papers, and Resolutions.
  • 03 External Conferences: Travel and compete as a unified school delegation across the GTA.
Pillar II • Hosted Conference Dates TBD

Brathwaite Model UN (BMUN)

Organized by the HMBSS Secretariat, BMUN is our flagship school-hosted conference welcoming delegates for rigorous parliamentary debate, dynamic crisis updates, and diplomacy. Official conference dates and committee background guides will be announced soon.

  • 01 Diverse Committee Slate: Featuring General Assemblies, Specialized Agencies, and Crisis Committees.
  • 02 Dedicated Secretariat: Directed by our 15-member Executive Team across 6 specialized departments.
  • 03 Delegate Recognition: Best Delegate gavels, Outstanding Delegate, Honourable Mention, and Best Position Paper awards.
